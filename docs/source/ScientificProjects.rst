.. _scientific_projects:

Scientific Projects
=====================================

AdOpT has been applied in several scientific research projects that have led to peer-reviewed publications.
Below is an overview of the main research topics addressed in these projects. Each topic links to a dedicated page
that provides a short description of the project, references to related publications, and links to the corresponding
repositories containing the respective AdOpT-NET0 versions.

----

.. _chemical_industry:

Chemical Industry Transformation
--------------------------------

The chemical industry is one of the most critical yet challenging sectors in the global transition toward net-zero emissions.
This research develops an open-source, multi-scale optimization framework to support strategic decision-making for transforming
chemical clusters toward net-zero. The framework integrates process-level, cluster-level, and system-level representations,
enabling consistent analysis of technology pathways, infrastructure needs, and policy interactions.
All model versions and supporting data are openly available as described below.

Ammonia-ethylene cluster emission reduction
^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^
Optimization of ammonia-ethylene clusters to identify cost-effective near-term emission
reduction strategies combining electrification, green hydrogen, and carbon capture.

:Publication: **Tiggeloven, J. L.**, Faaij, A. P. C., Kramer, G. J., & Gazzani, M. (2025).
   *Optimizing Emissions Reduction in Ammonia–Ethylene Chemical Clusters: Synergistic
   Integration of Electrification, Carbon Capture, and Hydrogen.* Industrial & Engineering
   Chemistry Research, 64, 4479–4497.
   DOI: `10.1021/ACS.IECR.4C03817 <https://doi.org/10.1021/ACS.IECR.4C03817>`_
:Repository: `AdOpT-NET0_AmmEth_EmisRed <https://github.com/julia1071/AdOpT-NET0_AmmEth_EmisRed>`_
:Zenodo: `10.5281/zenodo.17341246 <https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.17341246>`_

Long-term transition to net-zero clusters
^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^
Brownfield transition modeling to net-zero ammonia–olefin clusters, using a rolling
horizon approach to capture three sequential investment intervals and comparing
greenfield and brownfield configurations.

:Repository: `AdOpT-NET0_ClusterNet0_paper <https://github.com/julia1071/AdOpT-NET0_ClusterNet0_paper>`_
:Zenodo: `10.5281/zenodo.17340163 <https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.17340163>`_

National-cluster model linking framework
^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^
Integration of a national energy system model with a detailed chemical cluster model,
enabling consistent cross-scale analysis of emission reduction strategies.

:Repository: `AdOpT-NET0_ModelLink <https://github.com/julia1071/AdOpT-NET0_ModelLink>`_
:Zenodo: `10.5281/zenodo.17342086 <https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.17342086>`_
