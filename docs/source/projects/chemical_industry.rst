Chemical Industry Transformation
================================

The chemical industry is one of the most critical yet challenging sectors in the global transition toward net-zero emissions.
This research develops an open-source, multi-scale optimization framework to support strategic decision-making for transforming
chemical clusters toward net-zero. The framework integrates process-level, cluster-level, and system-level representations,
enabling consistent analysis of technology pathways, infrastructure needs, and policy interactions.
All model versions and supporting data are openly available as described below.

Ammonia-ethylene cluster emission reduction
^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^
Optimization of ammonia-ethylene clusters to identify cost-effective near-term emission reduction strategies combining
electrification, green hydrogen, and carbon capture.

Peer-reviewed publication:
**Tiggeloven, J. L.**, Faaij, A. P. C., Kramer, G. J., & Gazzani, M. (2025). *Optimizing Emissions Reduction in Ammonia–Ethylene Chemical Clusters: Synergistic Integration of Electrification, Carbon Capture, and Hydrogen.* Industrial & Engineering Chemistry Research, 64, 4479–4497. DOI:`10.1021/ACS.IECR.4C03817 <https://doi.org/10.1021/ACS.IECR.4C03817>`_

`Repository <https://github.com/julia1071/AdOpT-NET0_AmmEth_EmisRed>`_

`Zenodo archive <https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.17341246>`_

Long-term transition to net-zero clusters
^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^
Brownfield transition modeling to net-zero ammonia–olefin clusters, using a rolling horizon approach to capture three sequential investment intervals and comparing greenfield and brownfield configurations.

`Repository <https://github.com/julia1071/AdOpT-NET0_ClusterNet0_paper>`_

`Zenodo archive <https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.17340163>`_

National-cluster model linking framework
^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^
Integration of a national energy system model with a detailed chemical cluster model, enabling consistent cross-scale analysis of emission reduction strategies.

`Repository <https://github.com/julia1071/AdOpT-NET0_ModelLink>`_

`Zenodo archive <https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.17342086>`_
