OS: Operating strategy. Can be ignored.
T: Temperature in degree Celsius
RH: Relative humidity in percent
E_tot: Specific total energy demand in MJ/kg(CO2)
E_th: Specific thermal energy demand in MJ/kg(CO2)
E_el: Specific electric energy demand in MJ/kg(CO2)
CO2_Out: Total CO2 produced in one hour for a contractor volume of 1m3 in kg(CO2)/h/m3
Water_Out: Water adsorbed in kg(H2O)/kg(CO2)
adTime: Duration of adsorption time step in s
prodTime: Duration of production time step in s
pressure: Pressure (bar)
purgeDeltaT: Temperature difference purging step in Kelvin
prodTemp: Temperature during production step in Kelvin
purgeTime: Duration of purging step in s
airVolume: Volume flow of air in m3/s
cycleTime: Total duration of one cycle (in s)
Point: Point on the input-output line for one temperature-humidity combination
